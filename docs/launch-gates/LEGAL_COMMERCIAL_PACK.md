# Legal and commercial launch pack

**Status: BLOCKED-ON-HUMAN.** Qualified counsel and product/commercial owners must draft, review, approve, and publish these materials. This checklist is not legal advice or a substitute for approved agreements.

| Artifact | Required review |
|---|---|
| Terms of Service | Product scope, permitted use, customer responsibilities, warranty/liability, suspension and termination |
| Privacy Notice | Data categories, purposes, lawful basis/consent where applicable, rights, retention, subprocessors, international transfers |
| Data Processing Agreement | Controller/processor roles, instructions, security measures, breach notice, subprocessors, deletion/return, audit assistance |
| SLA / support policy | Service definitions, measurement windows, exclusions, support hours, response targets, remedies and escalation |
| Subprocessor list | Provider, service, data location, data categories, change notice and objection process |
| Vulnerability Disclosure Policy | Reporting channel, safe harbor boundaries, triage targets, disclosure coordination |
| Trust center | Approved security architecture, control descriptions, certifications only when actually issued, evidence request process |
| Status page | Availability components, incident communication owner, maintenance and incident update process |

Before publication, confirm company identity, governing law, regional privacy obligations, retention periods, breach workflow, marketing claims, security representations, and insurance requirements. Record counsel approval, version, effective date, owner, and next review date for each document. Do not describe controls as certified without valid certification evidence.
